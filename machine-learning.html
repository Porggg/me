Machine Learning, Geometrically

An interactive website I built to study the machine learning course (CSED/AIGS 515) at POSTECH. Every notion comes with 2D/3D scenes you can manipulate, the formulas, collapsible step-by-step proofs and links to the slides and textbooks.

  • Linear regression: least squares as a projection, MLE, overfitting, ridge & lasso, gradient descent, momentum, SGD.
  • Logistic regression: sigmoid and log-odds, cross-entropy, Newton's method (IRLS), softmax.
  • SVM: geometric margin, Lagrangian and duality, KKT, SMO, soft margin, polynomial and RBF kernels.
  • 44 exam-style exercises with detailed solutions.

Everything is computed in the browser by a small engine written from scratch: linear algebra (QR, Jacobi), solvers (Newton, SMO), a 2D plotting layer and a Three.js 3D renderer.
