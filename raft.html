Raft 2D

A 2D survival game inspired by Raft, made with Tkinter. You are stranded on a few raft tiles in the middle of the ocean and must expand your raft with what drifts by.

  • Grid-based movement with the keyboard and directional sprites.
  • A building menu: select a piece, check its cost, then click on the sea to place it on the grid.
  • Planks spawning randomly on the water.
  • Player stats and position saved to a file.

The project is on hold: the game loop needs threads to run the world while the player moves. It will be restarted.
